④ PROJECT COMMENTARY (our interpretation)

The passage invites reflection on giving sincere attention to a person who comes seeking guidance, regardless of status or ability. Applying it to inclusive learning is the team's educational reading, not a claim made by the text.

Visual learning

Text alternative: Two different paths, one solid and one dashed, lead to the same open book under a ring of light. It is symbolic only: no people are depicted, and it is not a historical reconstruction.

Reflection (not an assessment)

Answers stay on this device screen only and are not scored.

1. How can we show respect when someone approaches us to seek knowledge?
2. What assumptions might stop us from giving someone proper attention?
3. What practical action could make a learning space more inclusive?

Learning summary

  • Attention and respect are owed to anyone who comes seeking guidance.
  • Outward status should not decide how much care we give.
  • Practical ideas: ask about access needs, offer text, audio and visual versions, and involve persons with disabilities in design.

At this stage, the prototype demonstrates the proposed learning experience and its technical functionality. Its effects on empathy and learning outcomes have not yet been established through empirical evaluation.
